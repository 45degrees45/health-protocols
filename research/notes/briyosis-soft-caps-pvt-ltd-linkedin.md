About us
Briyosis is a privately-owned, FDA registered and WHO (World Health Organization) - GMP certified, ISO 9001: 2008, full service contract manufacturer of custom dietary supplement formulations. We feature high efficiency encapsulation lines operating 24/7, capable of producing over 3 billion soft-gels annually. 

Briyosis fully complies to FDA cGMP’s and has been independently certified by NAFDAC

Founded in 2006, Briyosis has grown to encompass 100,000 square feet of production, laboratory and warehousing space.
