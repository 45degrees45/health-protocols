Vapi Care Pharma Pvt. Ltd.
With more than 300 pharmaceutical dosage forms like tablets, capsules, dry powder for orals, liquid orals, ointment and creams, VCPPL is currently manufacturing partner for major multinational supporting more than 100 quality formulations. We operate in more than 18 countries and have more than 100 products registered across the globe. The company has earned client’s and customer’s support due to its commitment and consistency in fulfilling the needs and demands. Our small R&D capabilities include process optimization and formulation research & development.
