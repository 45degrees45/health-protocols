Product Description of Briyo Vitamin D3 2000Iu Capsule 90
Briyo Vitamin D3 2000 IU Capsules 90
is used for managing bone health, immune manage, and muscle function. Regular use helps maintain overall health and ensures the optimal functioning of various physiological systems. Ideal for individuals seeking to manage bone density and manage immunity.
Ingredients of Briyo Vitamin D3 2000Iu Capsule 90
Vitamin D3 (Cholecalciferol)
: Vitamin D3, also known as cholecalciferol, is a vital nutrient that manages calcium absorption and bone mineralization, ensuring strong and healthy bones. It plays a key role in immune system function, muscle health, and overall well-being. Regular intake of Vitamin D3 helps manage deficiencies, manages inflammation, and manages balanced calcium and phosphorus levels in the body.
Other Ingredients:
Gelatin, Soybean Oil, Purified water.
Key Uses of Briyo Vitamin D3 2000Iu Capsule 90
It Helps in Managing Bone Health
: Vitamin D3 manages calcium absorption in the gut, ensuring the development and maintenance of strong bones and teeth. It manages the risk of bone disorders like osteoporosis and fractures by managing bone density and managing overall skeletal health.
Helps in Managing Immune Health
: Vitamin D3 manages the immune system by managing the function of immune cells such as T-cells and macrophages. This helps the body manage infections more effectively and reduces the risk of diseases.
Assists in Managing Muscle Function
: Vitamin D3 plays a critical role in muscle strength and function by regulating calcium levels necessary for muscle contraction. Adequate levels of Vitamin D3 can manage the risk of muscle weakness, spasms, and falls, especially in older adults.
Maintains Overall Health
: Beyond bones and immunity, Vitamin D3 contributes to overall health by managing inflammation, cardiovascular function, and mental well-being. Regular supplementation helps maintain optimal levels, ensuring balanced and healthy bodily functions.
