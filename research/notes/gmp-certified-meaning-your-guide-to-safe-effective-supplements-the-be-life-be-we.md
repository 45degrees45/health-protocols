GMP vs. ISO Certified: The Crucial Difference for Your Supplements
You might see ISO 9001 mentioned. While good, ISO 9001 is a
general
quality management standard for any business.
GMP is specific to manufacturing supplements, drugs, and food.
GMP dictates the
actual practices
needed for product safety. For supplements,
GMP emerges as the critical certification
ensuring what's inside the bottle is safe and accurately made. A company can hold both
iso and gmp certified
status, but for your health supplements, prioritize GMP.
The Indian Scenario: What is a GMP Certificate in India?
Within India, GMP principles are generally referenced under Schedule M of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, relevant to pharmaceutical manufacturing quality and overseen by bodies associated with the CDSCO. A
GMP certificate in India
confirms a manufacturer has passed audits verifying compliance with these established Good Manufacturing Practices. Choosing brands manufactured in
gmp certified
facilities within India, like Be., gives you confidence in adherence to these recognized quality standards.
