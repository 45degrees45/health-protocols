FAQs
How are the FSSAI nutraceutical food laws?
These are regulations by FSSAI on the safety, content, labelling and approval of nutraceutical and functional food products in India.
Do nutraceutical products require an FSSAI license?
Yes, all the nutraceutical products have to receive an FSSAI license to manufacture or sell in India.
How do nutraceuticals differ from functional foods?
Nutraceuticals are supplements such as capsules or powders, whereas functional foods are ordinary products with additional health effects.
What is the time taken to obtain FSSAI approval?
The time of taking the license can be between 15 and 60 days, based on the type of license and the completeness of the documentation.
What are the most frequent causes of refusal of FSSAI applications?
Incorrect documentation
The use of ingredients that are not approved
Misleading claims
Non-compliant labeling
What should be tested on nutraceutical products?
Compliance needs to be done through stability testing, microbial testing, heavy metal analysis, and shelf-life studies.
Is it possible to sell nutraceutical products online without the FSSAI license?
No, it is not legal to sell without an FSSAI license, online and offline.
