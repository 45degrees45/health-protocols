FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What is a Vitamin D Supplement and who actually needs one?
Vitamin D supplements provide cholecalciferol (Vitamin D3) or ergocalciferol (Vitamin D2) to address the body’s needs. Over 50–90% of Indians are deficient due to low sun exposure and dietary gaps. ICMR-NIN recommends 600 IU daily for adults. Those with confirmed deficiency, limited sunlight, or high-risk conditions benefit most.
What is the recommended daily Vitamin D dosage for Indians?
ICMR-NIN recommends 600 IU (15 mcg) of Vitamin D daily for adults, including pregnant and lactating women. The Tolerable Upper Limit is 4000 IU per day. High-dose supplements above this threshold should only be taken under medical supervision to avoid toxicity.
What are the side effects of Vitamin D Supplements?
Vitamin D is fat-soluble and accumulates in the body. Excess intake may cause nausea, weakness, frequent urination, and hypercalcemia. ICMR-NIN's Tolerable Upper Limit is 2000 IU daily. Those with kidney disease, hypercalcemia, or sarcoidosis should avoid supplementing without medical clearance.
Should I take Calcium and Vitamin D Supplements together?
Yes. Vitamin D enhances calcium absorption in the gut, making them an effective pairing for bone health. ICMR-NIN recommends 600 IU Vitamin D and 1000mg calcium daily for adults. Before buying, check Unbox Health's independent lab-tested ratings to verify that products deliver their labelled values.
Do Vitamin D and K2 Supplements work better together?
Vitamin K2 (MK-7) helps direct calcium to bones and away from arteries, complementing Vitamin D's role in calcium absorption. Evidence is growing but ICMR-NIN has not set a formal K2 recommendation for Indians. Combined D3 and K2 products are available; consult a doctor before use.
When should you take Vitamin D Supplements for best results?
Vitamin D is fat-soluble, so take it with a meal containing healthy fats for better absorption. Morning or afternoon works best. Avoid taking it late at night as it may interfere with melatonin production and disrupt sleep quality in some individuals.
Can I take Vitamin D Supplements without consulting a doctor?
Many Indians supplement Vitamin D given widespread deficiency. However, the correct dose depends on your serum 25(OH)D levels in a blood test. ICMR-NIN recommends 600 IU daily for maintenance, but high-dose supplements of 5000 IU or more should only be taken under medical supervision.
Are Vitamin D Supplements safe for long-term use?
At ICMR-NIN's recommended 600 IU daily, Vitamin D is safe for long-term use. Doses consistently above the Tolerable Upper Limit of 4000 IU per day may cause toxicity over time. Get serum Vitamin D levels tested every 6 months if supplementing at higher doses.
How do I choose the best Vitamin D Supplement in India?
Look for
Vitamin D3
(cholecalciferol), which absorbs better than D2. Choose a dose matching your need: 600 IU for daily maintenance, higher only under medical guidance. Use Unbox Health's independent ratings based on NABL-accredited lab test results to verify whether a product's label claims are accurate before buying.
Are Vitamin D Supplements vegetarian or vegan?
Most Vitamin D3 supplements are derived from lanolin (sheep's wool) and are not strictly vegan, though lacto-vegetarians generally accept them. Plant-based D3 sourced from algae or lichen is vegan-friendly. Check Unbox Health's product ratings to find high-quality vegan Vitamin D Supplements available in India.
