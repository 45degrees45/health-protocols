FSSAI Registration For Food Supplements & Nutraceuticals In From C – Overview
FSSAI License For Food Supplements & Nutraceuticals. Form C covers various licenses, including manufacturing, marketing, importing (central), storage, distribution, wholesaler, retailer, transporter, and exporter. -
The
Food Safety and Standards Authority of India
(FSSAI) oversees food-related licenses in India. Anyone dealing with food supplements and nutraceuticals need a valid FSSAI License.
Food Business Operators (FBOs) have faced accusations of violating the Food Safety and Standards Act (FSSAI). It can be adulterating, producing or selling inferior food, and using hazardous ingredients. Suppose the FBOs follow the guidelines to get the licences. In that case, they cannot manufacture, supply, distribute, or import food products into India without a valid FSSAI licence.
Having an
FSSAI License
means the food is verified and safe to eat. It also means the food business is registered with FSSAI, making it easier to keep track of licenses and follow rules like safety audits, product testing, and recalls. This helps businesses ensure they're following FSSAI guidelines and making safe food. Following regulations ensures the food is safe, doesn't harm health, and lets consumers choose wisely while ensuring fair business competition.
The following process needs to follow to apply for the FSSAI License:
The applicant should apply in Form B.
They can submit the required documents and pre-requisite FSSAI Licensing fees to the Food Authority.
After applying, the FBOs receive a Unique Application Reference Number.
The Food Authority further scrutinises the submitted application.
The Licensing Authority tells FBOs to fix and complete their application within 30 days. If not, it may be rejected.
The Licensing Authority reviews the application and documents. They then send a Food Safety Officer to inspect the premises and issue a report.
After satisfactory scrutiny, the Licensing Authority grants the FSSAI License to the applicant within 60 to 90 days.
Who Can Apply?
Any Indian-based manufacturer, importer, distributor, retailer, wholesaler, or transporter involved in food-related business can apply for FSSAI License.
How To Apply?
The Applicant must follow the following  process:
To obtain FSSAI License, the applicant must follow the following procedure:
The applicant must visit the FoSCoS website for food licensing and registration.
The applicant is required to register on the FoSCoS and log in with a username, password and captcha.
The applicant must select Apply for New License/Registration" from the dashboard.
The applicant should fill in the required details based on the premise and KoBs.
The applicant completes Form B and attaches the required documents, such as an Aadhaar Card, Form IX, proof of premise, etc.
Preview the application and submit it to proceed with the payment.
Upon completing the payment, a receipt is generated with a 17-digit reference number for future reference.
After satisfactory scrutiny of the application and the submitted documents, the Food Authority grants the license. · The FBOs must follow the conditions laid down in the granted license.

Frequently Asked Questions
Are different Licenses required to conduct a different kinds of food business activities at the same premise?
FSSAI license is based on the premise. All kinds of food business activities that are being conducted at the same premise can be applied for in a single FSSAI license.
What is the FSSAI License requirement to run a food business in more than one state with the same name of the company or organisation?
If an FBO operates in multiple states, they need a Central License for their main office and separate licenses for each unit. Each unit needs a license based on its capacity or turnover from the relevant authorities.
At which points, an inspection of my food business will be conducted
Inspections are conducted on the points of Sanitary and Hygienic Requirements. They are mentioned in Schedule 4 of the FSS (Licensing and Registration of Food Businesses) Regulations, 2011. Kind of Business wise standardised inspection checklists used by inspectors are based on Schedule 4.
How will I know about the stages/status of my application?
An applicant is informed by SMS/Email alert at many application processing stages. Moreover, the applicant can obtain the status by using the "Track Application Status" facility on FoSCoS.
Is the FSSAI License under Importer KoB sufficient to carry out further food Business activities of Imported Food Products?
No, the FSSAI License under Importer KoB is only for
importing food
Items into the Country. Therefore, for carrying out further food business activities of imported food products, as defined under clause 3(n) of the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006. FBOs need to endorse all such relevant Kinds of Business categories in the issued license by applying a modification of the license.
