About us
Briyo Supplements was established with a passion to ensure that its consumers have an affordable and effective way to meet their daily nutritional requirements. Our mission is and will always remain to provide valued products to support healthy lifestyles, empowering the consumers to meet the challenges of the day to day tasks.

Consistency in quality and application of scientific research has formed the base for the brand, and has given Briyo Supplements unparalleled excellence. 

We pride in belonging to an industry which makes a difference in the lives of people. Today, we are a fully unified pharmaceutical company with a reach over 25 countries, built on a foundation of extensive research, advanced manufacturing facilities and an undeniably strong global supply chain. With these pillars of foundation in place, the future looks brighter than it has ever been.

Briyo Supplements’s approach at meeting unmet customer needs, ensuring a promising future is created on a base of state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities established in India. Operational rigour coupled with strong regulatory compliance serves as a sound foundation for the brand to deliver affordable and superior quality supplements worldwide.
