Key quantitative findings: oil-based vehicles produced the greatest mean rise in 25(OH)D per 100 IU/day (4.05 nmol/L per 100 IU/day), followed by powder-based vehicles (2.75 nmol/L per 100 IU/day), and ethanol (0.5 nmol/L per 100 IU/day).
