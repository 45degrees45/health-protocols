Why This Protocol

A well-planned vegan diet is powerful — but certain nutrients are harder to get in sufficient amounts from plants alone. This protocol fills those gaps with vegan-certified supplements and adds the same environmental hacks that benefit everyone. Every product on this list has been checked: vegan capsules, no lanolin-derived D3, algae-sourced Omega-3, plant-based everything.
