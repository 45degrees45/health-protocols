The 3 Label Claims — Evidence Grades
Boost Immune System
Martineau 2017 BMJ: 25 RCTs, 11,321 people. Daily D3 reduces respiratory infection risk (OR 0.81). Severely deficient subjects show OR 0.30. GRADE HIGH. Lead with this claim.
Strong ✓
Healthy Bone Function
72.5% of tested Indians are vitamin D deficient — for them, 2000 IU/day is well-supported for bone health. Attenuates in replete subjects (VITAL: no BMD improvement at baseline 25(OH)D 77 nmol/L). Frame as deficiency correction, not universal bone booster.
Solid *
Strong Muscles
LeBoff 2023 (n=136, exactly 2000 IU/day, 12 months): zero improvement in grip strength, leg power, or any other muscle measure — even after serum D3 confirmed to have risen. Do not make ergogenic claims.

Safe wording: "Helps maintain normal muscle function in those with vitamin D deficiency."
Null ✕
The One Big Gap
No independent lab test on file for the softgel

The brand claims "third-party tested" but names no lab and shows no Certificate of Analysis for this SKU. For context: HealthKart — India's #1 D3 by Amazon rank — was independently tested and found to contain 2.16× the labeled dose. Amazon rank and star ratings do not measure label accuracy. Briyo could be fine; we just can't prove it yet. The chewable is better documented — Lab Reports and CoA buttons exist on the brand website.
