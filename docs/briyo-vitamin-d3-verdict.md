## The 3 Label Claims — What the Evidence Says

### Boost Immune System — STRONG ✅
Martineau 2017 BMJ meta-analysis: 25 RCTs, 11,321 people. Daily D3 reduces respiratory infection risk. GRADE HIGH evidence. **Lead with this claim.**

### Healthy Bone Function — SOLID for most Indians ✅
72.5% of tested Indians are vitamin D deficient. At 2000 IU/day, correcting deficiency improves bone health. Frame it as *deficiency correction*, not a universal bone booster — doesn't help people who are already replete.

### Strong Muscles — DO NOT USE ❌
The best-matched trial (LeBoff 2023: 136 people, exactly 2000 IU/day, 12 months) found **zero improvement** in grip strength, leg power, or any other muscle measure — even after serum levels confirmed to have risen. Do not make ergogenic claims.

**Safe alternative wording:** *"Helps maintain normal muscle function in those with vitamin D deficiency"*
